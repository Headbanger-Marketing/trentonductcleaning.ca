Who Receives Your Request

Information for Your Trenton Quote

  • Where each vent ends

    Note which wall each hood or cap sits on, and roughly how high above the ground it is.

  • What you see there

    Describe any flap, screen or louvre, and whether it looks open, stuck or covered by lint, snow, a nest or plants.

  • Photos from a safe spot

    Take wide and close photos from the ground, standing well back from any ladder, roof edge or nest.

Ask the responding provider for a written scope, any exclusions, applicable qualifications and insurance, the expected visit window, and the terms of any warranty. Keep the estimate and invoice together. These service pages explain common work; they do not replace an inspection of your property.
